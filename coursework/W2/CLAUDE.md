## Week 2

The goal this week is to determine if the available data addresses the research question.

Falling for the wrong dataset is the fastest way to sink a project.

The trap: you find a big, clean, interesting dataset — and quietly bend your question to fit it. Now you're answering the data's question, not yours. The fix is to interrogate the data against the problem you already wrote — before you commit a single hour of analysis to it.

Interrogate any dataset with four questions:

1. **Fit:** Can it actually answer the project's research question — not a nearby one?
2. **Provenance:** Where did it come from, and can you trust how it was collected?
3. **Limits:** What can't it tell you? What's missing, biased, or stale?
4. **Feasibility:** Can you really get and work with it in the time you have?
