60 points
Focus this week: Choose & justify methods — pick an analysis you can defend over the obvious alternative.

Pod Lab — Defend your method (90 min)
With your pod
Name your method and why it fits your question and data. Ask an LLM for one alternative and read its case. In the pod challenge, each person defends their choice against the alternative while the pod pushes back. Write your one-paragraph defense — or switch, and say what changed your mind. Output: a short method justification (your choice, the alternative, why yours fits).
