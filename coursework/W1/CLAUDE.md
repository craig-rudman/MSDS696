## Week 1

### Deliverable 1.1: Project Proposal
- **Student's Name:**
- **The problem to address:**, in one sentence: The defensible question — specific, and answerable with data the student can actually get.
- **The three parts:**  If the student can't fill all three, then we have a topic, not a problem yet:
  - *The question*
  - *The stakeholder* (who acts on the answer):
  - *The action* (what the stakeholder would do differently once they have it):

- **Personal angle:** The domain, constraint, or population only they could bring; what makes this theirs to defend.
- **Candidate data:** What data would answer this, and where it comes from. Name the source and link it.
- **First milestones / rough plan:** The intended first few steps — data, cleaning, EDA, modeling. Tag DONE as the work progresses.

**How it's graded:** On the sharpness of the problem, the data source, and a sensible first plan — not on whether the project will succeed.

**How it's submitted:** The student should use the template provided, and submit to the assignments drop box.

### Deliverable 1.2: Practice Talk
- *Part 1:* The student is to record a short (about 5-minute) screen recording or live-session link delivering their one-slide assertion-evidence talk on their project's problem and the data they'll use. 
- *Part 2:* The student is to reply to at least one classmate with one thing that landed and one thing they'd push on. It should be constructive and specific.

### Deliverable 1.3: LLM Brainstorm
The student is to brainstorm and critique their problem statement with an LLM. They are to fill an LLM Collaboration Log Entry (the exchange, what it suggested, what you kept/rejected and why) and submit a copy of it to assignment dropbox.
